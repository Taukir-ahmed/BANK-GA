# September 2026 monthly replacement

Source: the user-supplied Smartkeeda **Smart Points PDF (September 2026)**,
144 PDF pages, with 464 numbered news items. The monthly content runs from
National & States Affairs on PDF page 4 through partnerships on PDF page 134.
The repeated daily-date header on the cover is not used to determine coverage;
the monthly title, index and complete news sections identify the source.

The replacement contains 368 questions drawn from 360 distinct news items.
It is an editorial drill bank covering the full month's subject sections,
not one question for every source item. Selection favours institutions,
locations, schemes, full forms, appointments and major outcomes over minor
events or amount/percentage-only recall. Eight two-statement questions revisit
selected facts in a different framing. Facts follow the supplied source;
they have not been independently verified against external publications.

Every question has a `source` object with the PDF document title, one-based
PDF page and numbered news item. The same locator appears in the answer note,
visible after answering in Practice and after submission in Test. Page numbers
refer to PDF page positions. The original PDF remains in the user's Downloads;
it is not bundled into the application.

The previous Week 1 sheets and their must-know reference map are preserved in
`docs/september-weekly-archive/` and are not auto-discovered by the application.
Monthly sheets use IDs `september-2026-monthly-29-v1` through
`september-2026-monthly-32-v1`. Their numeric `baseId` and `order` preserve
labels 29–32 and month order. Inline must-know flags replace weekly index
references. New regular and starred score keys keep old weekly results separate;
existing localStorage records are retained.
